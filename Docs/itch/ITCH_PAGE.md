# VERSO
### Flip the world. Cross to the other side.

*A cozy-spooky 3D puzzle platformer made in 2 days for a game jam. Theme: **The Other Side**.*

---

## The story

Lume wakes up on an island floating in the night sky, a little flame with no memory of how it got there.
A friendly lost soul called **Wisp** floats over and explains the strange rule of this place:

> *"These are the Verso Islands. Each one exists twice: in the world of the Living, and in the Beyond."*

Same place, never at the same time. A bridge that's here might vanish on the other side.
Many souls got lost between the two worlds, and only someone who can **flip the world** can bring them home.

And sometimes, Lume feels like someone is following, one step behind, on the other side...

*Who's there? You'll find out.*

---

## How to play

**Flip the world.** Press **F** and everything switches between the **Living** (warm sunset) and the **Beyond** (moonlit night).
See-through blocks belong to the other side: flip, and they become solid.

**Gather 3 souls.** Each island hides three lost souls. Some only exist in one world.

**Grab the key.** When all souls are found, a golden key appears at the portal. Take it to reach the next island.

**Flip in mid-air.** If something solid from the other side is right where you're standing, the world refuses to flip.
Jump first and press F while you're in the air: the platform appears under your feet.

**Mind the rhythm.** Spikes also live on one side only. Flip at the right moment to make the ones ahead disappear.

**Meet the Echo.** On the last island, a ghost repeats your every move 1.5 seconds later, always on the opposite side.
It can press the plates you can't reach. Plan your path for two.

**3 lives.** Falling or touching spikes costs a life. Lose all three and it's back to Island 1.

---

## Islands

1. **Awakening** – learn to flip.
2. **Crossing** – steps that switch sides.
3. **Leap** – flip in mid-air.
4. **Rhythm** – faster steps and spikes on both sides.
5. **Echo** – the other side was always you.

## Controls

| | Keyboard & mouse | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD | Left stick |
| Camera | Mouse | Right stick |
| Jump | Space | A |
| Flip the world | F / Right click | Y |
| Restart island | R | Select |
| Pause | Esc | Start |

**Settings** (gear icon): English / Português, music and effects volume.

---

## Features

- One mechanic explored to the end: two overlapping worlds, one button
- 5 handcrafted islands with growing difficulty
- Voiced intro in English and Portuguese, in-game tips from Wisp
- Hand-made 3D characters, PSX-style skies, cute spooky soundtrack
- About 10 minutes to finish
